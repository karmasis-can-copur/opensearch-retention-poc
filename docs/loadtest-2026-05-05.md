## 30k EPS Kapasite Notu

30k EPS x 86,400 saniye = 2,592,000,000 event/gun.

Bu kosuda 1,020,000 dokuman:

- Hot stable logical store: 1.72 GB
- Hot immediate post-ingest logical store: 2.16 GB
- Cold force-merged store: 1.52 GB
- Snapshot repo: 1.5 GB

Kaba lineer projeksiyon:

- 1 tam gun hot stable: yaklasik 4.4 TB primary store
- 1 tam gun hot immediate/merge-headroom: yaklasik 5.5 TB primary store
- 1 tam gun cold: yaklasik 3.9 TB primary store
- 1 tam gun frozen repo: yaklasik 3.8 TB

500 GB SSD'li mevcut test sunucusu 30k EPS icin tam 1 gun hot + 1 gun cold + 1 gun frozen tutmaya yetmez. Bu sunucu; lifecycle dogrulama, kisa 30k burst, producer tuning ve oran cikarma icin yeterli. Tam gun retention testi icin multi-TB disk gerekir.

   - Hot ingest icin ayri data node seti
   - Force-merge/cold icin ayri disk/node seti
